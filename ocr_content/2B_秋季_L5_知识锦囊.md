秋季英语 2B 知识锦囊 L5
剑二口语Part1
Part1 Find the Difference 找不同
Look at these pictures.
They look the same, but
some things are different.
In this picture, it's a
cloudy day, but in this
picture, it's a sunny day.
What other different
things can you see？
答题要求：
共有五处不同，考官一般会描述一处作为举例，考生描述剩余四处
1、用手分别指出两幅图片的不同
2、用完整的句子描述
•若回答cloudy；sunny 得一半分数你六
•若回答In this picture,it's cloudy, but in this
picture, it's sunny. 可得满分你六的
•若单词答错、未找出不同，不得分
参考答案：
1. In this picture,there is a dolphin, but in this picture, there is a boat.
2. In this picture,the snail is in front of/next to the bag, but in this picture, the snail is on the bag.
3.In this picture,the boy is wearing a redjacket, but in this picture,the boy is wearing a bluejacket.
4. In this picture, there are five shells, but in this picture, there are four shells.
