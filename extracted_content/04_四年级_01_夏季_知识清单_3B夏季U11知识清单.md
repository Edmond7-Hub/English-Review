## 第 3 页

HE 3B Unit 11 We always have fun! 知识点清单
n Hello Grammar-频率副词
always 总是
usually 通常
often 经常
sometimes 有时
never 从不
用法：
1.频率副词用在be动词之后，实义动词之前。
2.将don’t/doesn’t放在always，usually，often前，表示否定含义。
例句：
1.He is never tired.
2. Connor usually travels for competitions.
3. I don’t often go to basketball matches.
4. He doesn’t always take a shower.
