Day 8：第三人称单数与喜好 (Grade 2 Focus)
1. 核心词汇 (Vocabulary)
• Nature & Objects: donkey (驴), poster (海报), sunglasses (墨镜), tree (树)
• Adjectives: cute (可爱的), cool (酷的)
2. 重点语法 (Grammar)
• 第三人称单数提问 (Does he...?):
o 当主语是 He / She / It / 单个人名（如 Tom）时，助动词用 Does。
o 口诀: 见Does 动词用原形 (Does he like...? 不用 likes)。
• 句型结构:
o
问:
Does he like trees?
(他喜欢树吗？)
o 答: Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
3. 必背例句 (Sentences)
• Does the donkey like apples? (这头驴喜欢苹果吗？)
• Yes, it does. (是的，它喜欢。)
• He doesn't like sunglasses. (他不喜欢墨镜。)
• Does she like that poster? (她喜欢那张海报吗？)

Day 9：现在进行时 (Grade 2 Focus)
1. 核心词汇 (Vocabulary)
• Clothes (衣物): skirt (短裙), shirt (衬衫), coat (外套), glasses (眼镜), watch (手表)
• Verbs: wear (穿/戴) -> wearing
2. 重点语法 (Grammar)
• 现在进行时 (be doing):
o
含义: 表示此时此刻正在发生的动作。
o 结构: be动词 (am/is/are) + 动词ing
o
be动词口诀: 我(I)用am，你(You)用are，is 连着他(He)她(She)它(It)。
• 穿戴的表达:
o I am wearing a skirt. (我正穿着一条裙子。)
o He is wearing a watch. (他正戴着一块手表。)
3. 必背例句 (Sentences)
• What are you doing? I am playing. (你在做什么？我在玩。)
• She is wearing a red coat. (她穿着一件红外套。)
• They are wearing glasses. (他们戴着眼镜。)

## 第 7 页

Day 11：比较级进阶规则 (Grade 3 Focus)
1. 核心词汇 (Vocabulary)
• Adjectives: big (大的), fat (胖的), thin (瘦的), heavy (重的), happy (快乐的), funny (滑稽
的)
2. 重点语法 (Grammar)
• 比较级拼写规则 (Spelling Rules):
1. 双写规则: 词尾是"辅音+元音+辅音"的短单词 -> 双写末尾字母 + er。
§ big -> bigger
§ fat -> fatter
§ thin -> thinner
2. 改 y规则: 以"辅音+y"结尾 -> 变y为 i + er。
§ heavy -> heavier
§ happy -> happier
3. 不规则变化 (必背):
§ good (好) -> better (更好)
§ bad (坏) -> worse (更坏)
3. 必背例句 (Sentences)
• The elephant is bigger than the tiger. (大象比老虎大。)
• You are happier than me. (你比我快乐。)
• My English is better than my Maths. (我的英语比数学好。)

Day 12：最高级初步 (Grade 3 Focus)
1. 核心词汇 (Vocabulary)
• Superlatives: the best (最好的), the worst (最坏的), the biggest (最大的), the happiest (最快
乐的)
2. 重点语法 (Grammar)
• 最高级 (Superlatives):
o
含义: 三者或三者以上比较，“最...的”。
o
标志: 前面一定要加 the，词尾加 est。
o
变化规则 (同比较级):
§ tall -> the tallest
§ big -> the biggest (双写)
§ happy -> the happiest (变y为i)
• 句型: Who is the tallest? (谁最高？)
3. 必背例句 (Sentences)
• He is the tallest boy in the class. (他是班里最高的男孩。)
• Summer is the hottest season. (夏天是最热的季节。)
• This is the best book. (这是最好的书。)

Day 13：一般过去时与天气 (Grade 3 Focus)
1. 核心词汇 (Vocabulary)
• Weather (天气): sunny (晴朗的), cloudy (多云的), windy (刮风的), rainy/wet (下雨的/潮
湿的), dry (干燥的)
• Time (时间): yesterday (昨天), last week (上周)
2. 重点语法 (Grammar)
• 一般过去时 (be动词):
o
含义: 描述过去的状态。
o
变化:
§ am / is -> was
§ are -> were
• 句型:
o It is hot today. -> It was hot yesterday.
o They are happy now. -> They were happy last week.
3. 必背例句 (Sentences)
• It was sunny yesterday. (昨天是晴天。)
• We were at the zoo last Sunday. (上周日我们在动物园。)
• I wasn't (was not) happy. (我当时不开心。)

2. 易错语法总结
• Like的家族:
o Like + s (He likes)
o Do you like...? / Does he like...?
o Like doing (enjoy doing, love doing)

o Now (现在): am / is / are

o good -> better -> the best
o big -> bigger -> the biggest
