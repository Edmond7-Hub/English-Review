## 第 2 页

HE 3B Unit 12 Skills in Use 4 知识点清单
n Hello Grammar-Review U10 一般现在时
I / You / We / They + do ...
（ do代表动词原形） 遇到“小助手“
e.g. I like wearing dancing shoes.
(助动词Do/Does/don't/doesn't)
肯定句
He / She/ It + does ...
（ does代表动词三单形式）
e.g. He watches a cartoon with his friends. 后面动词现原形
I / You / We / They + don’t do ...
e.g. I don’t like wearing dancing shoes.
否定句
He / She/ It + doesn’t do ...
e.g. He doesn’t watch a cartoon with his friends.
Do I / you / we / they do ...? Yes, I / you / we / they do.
简短
一般 e.g. Do you like wearing dancing shoes? No, I / you / we / they don’t.
疑问句 Does he / she/ it + do ... ? 回答 Yes, he / she / it does.
e.g. Does he watch a cartoon with his friends? No, he / she / it doesn’t.
you提问，I或we回答
Wh- + do / does + person / thing + do ?
特殊
e.g. When do they read magazines?
疑问句
When does Tom go out with his dog?

## 第 3 页

HE 3B Unit 12 Skills in Use 4 知识点清单
n Hello Grammar-Review U11 频率副词
always 总是
usually 通常
often 经常
sometimes 有时
never 从不
用法：
1.频率副词用在be动词之后，实义动词之前。
2.将don’t/doesn’t放在always，usually，often前，表示否定含义。
例句：
1.He is never tired.
2. Connor usually travels for competitions.
3. I don’t often go to basketball matches.
4. He doesn’t always take a shower.
